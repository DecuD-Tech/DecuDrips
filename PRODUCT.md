# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Developers, open-source maintainers, and sponsors working within resilient or web3-adjacent ecosystems. They value utility, density, and transparent metrics over marketing fluff.

## Product Purpose
DocuDrip is a continuous documentation micro-funding dashboard. It visualizes real-time "drip" funding to open-source contributors based on their documentation efforts, simulating smart contract mechanics on the client side.

## Brand Personality
Dark Tech, Industrial, Focused, High-Utility.

## Anti-references
- No generic "AI Purple / Blue glow" gradients.
- No warm beige/cream/sand backgrounds.
- No overly rounded, bouncy, or "playful" UI elements (avoid `border-radius: 32px` or excessive glassmorphism).

## Design Principles
- **Data over Decoration**: Let the numbers speak. Use crisp layout and high contrast.
- **Hardware/Terminal Feel**: Interfaces should feel like precision instruments (e.g., standardized 8px radii, flat elevation, singular high-contrast accent color).
- **Restrained Motion**: Animate for feedback and state changes, not for infinite looping decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast minimums for all text and interactive elements.
- Honor reduced-motion preferences.
